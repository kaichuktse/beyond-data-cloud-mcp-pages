# Augmented Staffing Plan

## Overview

The **Augmented lane** uses the same committed team and 24-week calendar as Traditional, but captures AI tooling efficiency gains during delivery.

- **Timeline:** 24 weeks (committed, same as Traditional)
- **Team size:** Same roster as Traditional (10 PS resources)
- **Hours:** 7,452 (10% reduction from Traditional's 8,280 hours)
- **FTE:** ~7.7 FTE program-average
- **Key difference:** AI tools amplify productivity — same people, same duration, faster/smoother delivery

## Team Roster

All roles are the same as Traditional. Hours per resource drop by ~10% due to:
- Faster code generation and iteration (AI-assisted development)
- Automated testing and QA support
- Smarter debugging and defect resolution
- AI-powered config and integration assistance

**Disciplines:**
- **Program Management:** 1 PM (both workstreams, 24 weeks full-time)
- **Architecture:** 1 Solution Architect (Data 360 lead, full-time) + 1 Technical Architect (MCP Next lead, full-time)
- **Development:** 1 senior developer (integration lead) + 2 regular developers (volume pod, BigQuery/enrichment/connectors/Braze)
- **MCP Remediation:** 1 senior MCP developer (BBY US/Overstock fixes + BuyBuyBaby instrumentation)
- **Quality Assurance:** 1 QA lead (full-time on both workstreams) + 1 QA engineer (Data 360 activation/media/web/email volume)
- **Configuration & Stories:** 1 Functional Consultant (half-time, config/story authoring)

**Geographic split:** 6 offshore (build + QA) + 4 onshore (PM, Architects, Consultant)

## Why Augmented?

Augmented is the **safest step toward AI delivery** without restructuring the team:

- **No org change:** Same team, same roles, same accountability
- **Same calendar:** Committed 24-week timeline (no compression risk)
- **Lower risk:** Team stays familiar with the engagement scope
- **Measurable gain:** 10% efficiency (conservative from the 6–16% Low-readiness band) unlocks ~828 hours of buffer or smoother execution
- **Enabler for AI-Native:** Proves the team can work well with AI tools, validating readiness for the more aggressive AI-Native model later

## When to Choose Augmented

- **You want AI efficiency gains without restructuring the team**
- **Your org isn't ready for AI-native roles (orchestrators, agents)**
- **You want to de-risk the move to AI by using familiar faces + tools**
- **You value the same calendar commitment as Traditional (24 weeks)**
