**Activation Target**

* Marketing Cloud Personalization
* Question: What is the timing and types of audiences you would like to propose from Data360?
  + Near-real time event based communications (per user event)
  + Scheduled segment audiences
  + Product purchase/browse signals from entity A to activate to entity B
  + Value segmentation
  + Lifecycle (based on account create, last purchase, etc)
* Paid Marketing
  + Question: How many target segments are required for paid marketing channels?
  + Per platform we’ll have:
    - Purchasers for Lookalike (Value, Recency, etc)
    - Purchasers non-marketing email subscribers
    - Category, typal purchasers
    - Seasonal holiday purchasers (for lookalike)
    - New Movers
    - College (aged or parent of)
    - Expectant families
  + Question: Besides Google Adwords and Facebook, are there any other paid media channels? (add the following media channels Tiktok, Pinterest)
    - Criteo, Pinterest, TikTok
